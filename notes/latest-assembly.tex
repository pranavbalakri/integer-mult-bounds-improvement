\section{Two assembly choices}
Set $\beta=1/16$, $x=2$, $y=\epsilon$, $\tau=1-a_b$ and $\sigma=1-a_c$.
Then $\sigma<\tau$, the internal exponent is $\chi=\tau$, and
\[
 (1-\beta)a_c=0.00003717212625>a_b=0.000036943733.
\]
For the respective gap $g$ below, set
$\lambda=\tau+g$ and $\lambda'=\tau+2g$.
The exact assembly choices are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
 &\epsilon&\delta&g&\kappa\\\hline
 \text{higher saving}&24999/25000&10^{-6}&10^{-11}&36942/10^9\\
 \text{lower overhead}&993/1000&1/500&10^{-9}&3668/10^8
\end{array}
\]
Both exceed $\kappa_0$. The respective relative improvements are approximately
$0.753687\%$ and $0.0391222\%$.

The inherited cost margins, now with $(1+x)\delta=3\delta$, are
\[
 1-\epsilon,\quad \epsilon(1-\lambda'),\quad
 1-\epsilon-3\delta,\quad\epsilon,\quad\epsilon a_b.
\]
They cover prefix moves, simultaneous butterflies, fine-field/Gaussian/chirp/
reserved-axis work, packed products, and CRT reversal, respectively.
Each margin strictly exceeds its chosen $\kappa$. The butterfly row binds,
with exact remaining gaps
\[
 \frac{23525148}{10^{17}}>0
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 \frac{3140869}{10^{15}}>0.
\]
The fine-field/Gaussian/chirp/reservation margins themselves are $0.000037$
and $0.001$. All other constraints also hold:
\[
 \lambda'>\max(\lambda,\sigma+\beta(1-\sigma)),\quad\lambda'<1,
 \quad\epsilon<\frac1{2-\lambda'},
\]
\[
 \epsilon C_1<3,\qquad 2\epsilon+y=3\epsilon<3,
 \qquad y\ge\epsilon,\quad0<\delta<1/8.
\]
Choose $\mu=(1-\epsilon)/6$ in the record-size condition; then
$\mu(1+x)=(1-\epsilon)/2<1-\epsilon$.
The independent exact-rational certificate reports every constraint and margin.

\paragraph{Effect of the precision prefactor.}
The new coefficient length is $b'=128b^3$, and $p=6b'$.
The usual digit choice gives $T\in[4n/b',8n/b')$ and therefore
$Tp=\Theta(n)$, while
$L=\log_2T=b-3\log_2b+O(1)\sim b$.
In the nontrivial digit regime $L\le b$; smaller inputs use a base procedure.
The inherited coefficient capacity and final-error proof uses the same
$b'$ and $p=6b'$, so it is unaffected by the fixed prefactor.
The explicit Gaussian bound in the guard section establishes
$\alpha^2\theta_i>1$ and $2d\alpha^2\le b'/4$.

\paragraph{Other thresholds remain eventual.}
The segmented-inverse setup requires $\lambda_{\rm block}^2P\le s_i$.
Here $\lambda_{\rm block}^2=O(b^{3\epsilon})$, $P=O(b^3)$ and
$s_i=2^{\Theta(b^{1-\epsilon})}$, so it still holds eventually.
Likewise the record and prime-selection hypotheses are asymptotic statements,
with the fixed prefactors absorbed by their strict exponent gaps.
This argument gives no finite crossover for the complete algorithm.

\paragraph{Overhead comparison.}
The comparator chooses $1-\epsilon\simeq0.0000366657$;
the lower-overhead profile uses $1-\epsilon=0.007$.
Thus the power controlling the axis width increases by approximately $191$.
Its arithmetic allowance is $\delta=0.002$, instead of approximately
$6.8\cdot10^{-27}$ in the comparator, and its two strict exponent gaps are
$10^{-9}$ instead of $10^{-16}$.
The guard exponent is one, and the coefficient precision is cubic rather than
of degree $14696$. These comparisons address distinct overhead requirements;
they are not multiplicative runtime speedups. In particular the $h=16$
complex recursion already needs at least $256$ active axes, and the bit
recursion uses dimension $529$. The complete construction remains galactic.

\section{Reproduction, limitations and earlier work}
Run \texttt{python3 research/verify.py} from the accompanying repository using
Python 3.10 or newer. It uses only the standard library. Eighteen required
upstream files are included unchanged and checked against a SHA-256 manifest
pinned to the source commit. The new circuit, moments, scalar guards and
assembly checks are regenerated into \path{certificates/latest.json}.
The checker includes nine unit tests with negative controls, all 2,047 small
omitted-sum zero patterns, all 40,077 bit slot chains, 1,970 local rank checks,
all 313,600 complex matrix coefficients, and 33,956 complex frame checks.
A complete smaller scalar network restores 100,912 dirty scratch values
across 112 invocations; an incorrectly timed retained copy fails its control.

These checks concern exact arithmetic and finite identities. They do not
constitute formal verification of the inherited multiplication theorem,
independent review of this proposal, or a timing benchmark. In particular
no concrete rational bit basis or end-to-end fixed-tape implementation is
claimed by this bundle.

The earlier signed-rectangle extension with $\kappa=59/10^{11}$ is preserved
in \path{notes/archive/complex-rectangles-note.tex} and its existing manuscript
patch. That older patch does not implement the extension in this note.
The present construction is specified by this note and its reproducible
research sources, with the inherited assumptions listed at the beginning.
